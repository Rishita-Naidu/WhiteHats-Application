Q. Why I want to join WhiteHats?
So, as most of our lives have moved online, protecting our digital identities and personal information have become just as important as protecting ourselves in the real world. The reason I want to join WhiteHats is to learn about ethical hacking and develop the skills that are required to identify vulnerabilities in the applications or websites we use and help prevent them from being exploited. The thing that piques my interest the most is the idea that these skills can be used to protect people who may not know how vulnerable their information is. I want to understand how these attacks happen, not just to recognise them, but to help stop them. 
One cybersecurity news/incident you recently read about
One cybersecurity incident I recently read about involved a student named Nisarga who claimed that he had found security weaknesses in the CBSE digital evaluation portal. He shared a video showing the song *Bad Apple* playing on the portal using SQL injection. This made me curious about how weaknesses in websites can be misused and how developers can prevent them.
